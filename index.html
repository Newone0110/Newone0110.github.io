Computer VisionIn progress

Food Ingredient Detection for Menu Recommendation

A YOLO11 object detector that recognizes 50 ingredients from a fridge or kitchen photo — the core of a web app that suggests recipes from what you already have.

  • Found data leakage in the public split (455 photos in multiple splits) and re-split 80/10/10 per original photo, stratified by class.
  • Picked the confidence threshold with F-β (β=1.5) to favor recall — missing an ingredient hurts more than a false alarm.
  • Reproducible pipeline: per-run configs, training queue, experiment log, unit tests, model card. 166 ms/photo on CPU.

Regression

Jabodetabek House Price Prediction

End-to-end price model from 3,553 scraped rumah123.com listings, cleaned down to 3,172 real residential ads.

  • Group-aware split + GroupKFold so near-identical re-posted listings never leak across train and test.
  • Tuned XGBoost beat Ridge, RF & LightGBM: test MAPE 20.3%. SHAP shows building & land size drive price most.
